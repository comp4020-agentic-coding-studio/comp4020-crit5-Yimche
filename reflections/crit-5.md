# Crit 5

## What was the breakthrough that moved the work forward?

Truth be told, there wasn't much of a breakthrough with this one.
I guess it was moreso that I just knew what I wanted to do.
I love old school tech and games.
Rogue, Nethack, MUDs and text based adventures.
I think these are great.
So when it was time to make a game in the web browser I knew what I wanted to
make: An old school text-based adventure rpg.

Knowing made directing it very easy, as I knew exactly what kind of story beats
and fun moments I wanted to include.

## What did this work change about who I want to be as a software developer?

In this sense I want to make it so that I can enjoy the things I make.
I like the idea of making things that other people enjoy, but I think I do it
for myself, both the process and the play.
Making things like this I want the motivation to make it better and enjoying
what I make feels like that is the way
